    What is a Google review link?

    A Google review link is a web address that opens the "write a review" box for one business on Google. Your customer taps it, picks the stars and writes a few words. They don't have to search for you or scroll through Maps first.

    The link looks like this: https://search.google.com/local/writereview?placeid= followed by your business's Place ID. Google gives every business listing a Place ID, and this tool finds yours from your business name.

    How to get your Google review link

    With this tool (about 20 seconds)

    1. Type your business name and city in the box above.
    2. Pick your business from the list.
    3. Copy your link. Open it once on your phone to check it shows your business and the stars.

    Inside your Google Business Profile

    If you manage the profile, Google can also give you a link. Sign in, search for your business on Google or open the Google Maps app, open your profile, and choose Ask for reviews (sometimes shown as Get more reviews). Google gives you a short g.page link and a QR code. Both links open the same review box, so use whichever is easier. This tool also works when you don't have access to the profile, for example if a web person or a past agency set it up.

    What to say when you ask

    Keep it short and personal. Use their first name, say what you did, and send the link in the same message. Use "Copy a text to send" above for a ready-made message with your link in it.

    Ask every customer the same way. Most people who mean to leave a review simply forget, so one friendly reminder the next day brings in more reviews than any other trick.

    Mistakes that cost you reviews

    • Only asking happy customers. Google calls this review gating and does not allow it. That includes tools that ask for a star rating first and only send 4 and 5 star customers to Google. Ask everyone.
    • Offering something for a review. A discount or gift card in exchange for a review breaks Google's rules, and Google can remove those reviews.
    • Sending a Maps link instead of a review link. A Maps link opens your listing, and many people never find the review button. The review link opens the box itself.
    • Waiting a week. By then the job is a memory. Ask the same day.
    • Linking the wrong location. If you have more than one location, each has its own link. Test each one.

    If the link does not work

    • Your business does not show up in the search. Add your city or ZIP code. If it still does not show, paste the link from Google Maps (open your business, tap Share, then Copy link) using "Paste it instead".
    • The wrong business opens. You picked a similar name. Search again and check the address under each result.
    • The customer is asked to sign in. That is normal. Google only takes reviews from people with a Google account. Most Android phones are already signed in.
    • The listing is brand new. A profile that Google has not finished verifying may not show up yet. Try again after it is verified.

    Questions owners ask

    Is this Google review link generator free?

    Yes. There is no sign-up and no limit on how many links you make. We only use the business name you type to find the listing on Google.

    Do I need to own the Google Business Profile?

    No. The review link works for any business listed on Google, so you can make it even if a web person or an old agency set up the profile. Only the owner can reply to reviews, though.

    Do customers need a Google account to leave a review?

    Yes. Google only takes reviews from people signed in to a Google account. Most Android phones are signed in already, and iPhone users are asked to sign in when they open the link.

    Can I make a link that only lets people leave 5 stars?

    No. No link can do that, and Google does not allow asking only happy customers for reviews (review gating). Ask every customer the same way.

    Will my review link stop working?

    It keeps working as long as your Google listing exists. The link goes straight to Google, not through our servers, so it does not depend on us. If you move or Google merges your listings, make a new link here.

    Is this different from the link in my Google Business Profile?

    Both open the same review box. Google's own link starts with g.page and you need to sign in to the profile to get it. This link is built from your Place ID and works without signing in.

    What if I have more than one location?

    Each location has its own listing and its own review link. Search for each one, and check the address under the name before you pick it.
