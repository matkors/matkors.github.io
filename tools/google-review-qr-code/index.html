    What a Google review QR code does
    

    A Google review QR code is a square code that opens your business's review box when someone points a phone camera at it. It holds your Google review link, so the customer goes straight to the stars without searching for you.
    

    The code this tool makes points straight to Google. It does not pass through our servers, so it keeps working even if you never come back here, and it does not expire.

    How big to print it
    

    A simple rule: a QR code scans from about 10 times its own width. A 1 inch code works from about 10 inches away. A code on a truck that people scan from 10 feet away needs to be about 12 inches wide.

    Business card or invoice0.9 to 1.2 inHand distance

    Keep a white border around the code (the tool adds one) and print it black on white. Low contrast codes, like gray on blue, fail on older phones.

    What Google allows
    

    Google offers review QR codes itself, so using one is fine. Two things are not allowed: offering a discount or gift for a review, and asking only the customers you think are happy. Hand the card to everyone.
    


    Questions owners ask
    

    Is the Google review QR code free?
    Yes. The QR code, the cards and the sign are free with no sign-up. Print as many as you like.
    Does the QR code expire?
    No. It holds your review link directly and does not pass through our servers, so it works for as long as your Google listing exists.
    What size should I print the QR code?
    A QR code scans from about 10 times its width. About 1 inch is right for a business card, 3 to 4 inches for a counter sign, and 10 to 12 inches for a truck.
    Can I put my logo and colors on the card?
    Yes. Pick a color or your own brand color and upload your logo. The logo stays in your browser and is not uploaded anywhere.
    What paper do the cards print on?
    The sheet holds 10 cards in the Avery 8371 and 5371 business card layout. On plain card stock, keep the cut lines on and cut them apart.
    Does Google allow review QR codes?
    Yes. Google offers review QR codes itself. Just do not offer a reward for reviews, and hand the card to every customer, not only the happy ones.
