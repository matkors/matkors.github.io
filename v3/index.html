Flock Bio↗

High-throughput pooled DNA libraries  ·  lead generation  ·  Apify + n8n + Clay

No job title reliably identifies a scientist about to build a DNA library. So the system stopped looking for people and started looking for conversations.

42.6% reply rate on cold LinkedIn outreach, roughly 10x the benchmark for the channel.
  • Apify scrapes posts against 19 technical phrases each week. An LLM keeps the genuine industry discussion and throws out the recruiters and press releases.
  • Everyone who engaged with those posts is deduplicated by intent, then enriched and scored in Clay.
  • HeyReach sends one opener to the person who wrote the post and a different one to the people who engaged with it.
