Flock Bio

High-throughput pooled DNA libraries  ·  lead generation  ·  flockbio.com ↗

The list was small and the wrong message would burn it. So the system spends its effort on targeting and enrichment instead of send volume.

The problem

Flock Bio sells pooled DNA libraries to a narrow technical audience: people running CRISPR screens, protein and enzyme engineering, MPRAs, CAR-T libraries. That is not a market you can spray.

The usual outbound playbook is volume plus templating, and it fails here twice over. The addressable list is small enough that burning it has a real cost, and the buyers are technical enough that a generic message marks you immediately as someone who does not understand what they do.

The system

  2. 01Apifysource the raw list
  3. 02n8norchestration and sequencing logic
  4. 03Clayenrich until the message can say something true
  5. 04LinkedInsequenced outreach, deliberately low volume
  6. 05Reply trackingoutcomes feed back into targeting

Enrichment is the expensive step and the one that earns the reply rate.

Apify builds the raw prospect list. n8n orchestrates and handles sequencing. Clay enriches each prospect far enough that the opening line can reference something specific and accurate about their actual work rather than their job title. Replies are tracked back so targeting improves across the campaign instead of repeating.

The design principle: one message that proves you read their work beats forty that prove you did not.
