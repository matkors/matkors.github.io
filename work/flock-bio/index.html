01

Why searching by job title does not work

  • No job title tells you who is about to build a DNA library. Searching by title and company returns thousands of people, and almost none of them have the problem right now.
  • The audience is small enough that burning it with generic outreach cannot be undone. There is no second list.
  • What someone posted about last week does tell you. It is the only signal available that shows what they are working on rather than what they are called.
